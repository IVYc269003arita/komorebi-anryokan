Onsen etiquetteA step-by-step guide for your first visit
  1. Undress in the changing room

    Leave your clothes, swimwear and phone in a locker. You can take a small towel into the bathing area.

  2. Wash at a shower station

    Before getting into the bath, sit on a stool at a shower station. Wash and rinse off all soap and shampoo.

  3. Keep your towel and hair out of the water

    Set your towel on the edge of the bath and tie up long hair. Step in slowly and soak quietly.

  4. Dry off before returning to the changing room

    Use your small towel to dry off before leaving the bathing area so the changing room floor stays dry.

Frequently asked questions

Are towels available at the hot spring baths?

Bring a towel from your room. You can collect extra towels at the front desk.

Can I use the baths if I have a tattoo?

Please ask the front desk before using our baths if you have a tattoo.

Can I leave my luggage after check-out?

The front desk can hold your bags for collection later the same day.

How do I reach the front desk?

Dial 9 from your room phone.
